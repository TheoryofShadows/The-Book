Public-domain texts throughout: the World English Bible with Deuterocanon, R. H. Charles's 1917 Enoch and Jubilees, and the 1885 Ante-Nicene Fathers. Nothing here is drawn from a modern copyrighted translation.

The order is a scholarly reconstruction and is contested at many points. The accuracy report sets out what was verified, what was corrected, and what is still missing.
